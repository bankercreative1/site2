## SECTION 9 — Frequently Asked Questions

**How does payment work?**
Every site comes with three ways to pay: once as a project, over 12 months, or over 24 months. The website and the work are identical on every option; only the payment changes. There are no setup fees.

**Do I own my site?**
Yes. Pay for it as a project and it's yours at launch. Pay monthly and the website is paid off at the end of your term. After that, you choose what comes next: continue the search program, or drop to hosting and support only. [MATT TO CONFIRM: public language for canceling or paying off mid-term under the new model]

**What platform is it built on?**
We build on WordPress, hosted on a WordPress-optimized server, with a premium page builder for a flexible setup. We don't use closed, proprietary platforms, because you should *want* to work with us, not feel like you *have* to.

**Can I make updates myself?**
Yes. The sites are easy to update, so you can confidently make minor changes yourself. You also have a support team: submit a ticket and we'll change content, add photos, or publish a blog post for you.

**What does the search program include?**
Ongoing monthly work aimed at whatever matters most for getting your business found: local SEO, organic search, AI answers optimization, and site speed, plus a short monthly meeting to review progress.

**Is SiteFlyer a USA-based web design company?**
SiteFlyer is a brand of Banker Creative, based in St Paul, MN. We work with clients across the United States.
