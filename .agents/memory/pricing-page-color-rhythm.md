---
name: Pricing page color rhythm
description: The required background-color rhythm for visually separating major pricing-page sections.
---

Use alternating light and navy full-width section bands on the pricing page. A single background color across consecutive sections is not acceptable, even when individual cards use different colors.

**Why:** The user wants clear visual separation and explicitly clarified that the problem was not the navy color itself, but the page reading as one continuous color.

**How to apply:** Preserve a light pricing area, a navy Add-ons band, and a light Fine Print area. When changing section backgrounds, verify the rhythm across the whole page rather than judging one component in isolation.
