---
name: Nonprofit copy approval
description: The nonprofit landing page must use supplied draft copy only; do not add, paraphrase, or expand visible marketing copy without approval.
---

Visible nonprofit page copy must come from the supplied draft or remain explicitly marked for client confirmation. Do not invent extra hero lines, claims, names, prices, or service terms.

**Why:** The page is a client review draft, and unapproved marketing language changes the content the client is reviewing.

**How to apply:** Before editing nonprofit page copy, compare the proposed text with the attached draft and preserve its wording unless the user explicitly requests a copy change.
