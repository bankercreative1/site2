The (Not So) Fine Print

We are committed to being fully transparent about our pricing and our process. These are important details we want you to know before starting to work with us.

Strategy & Set-Up Fee

All website packages include a $1,750 setup fee, covering initial development and planning costs. This ensures we can deliver the high-quality work your project deserves.

Production Schedule

To maintain quality and stay on schedule, we limit the number of projects we take on each month. Once you reserve your spot, you'll pay the setup fee and schedule two key dates:

  1. Intro Call – Meet your project manager.
  2. Kickoff Date – Start your project.

Most websites take about 3 months from kickoff to launch. Your first subscription fee will be charged on your kickoff date.

Contract Length

All website packages require a 24-month minimum subscription. You can cancel anytime and walk away. If you'd like to keep your website before 24 months, simply buy out the remaining contract. After 24 months, you can cancel and take your site with no extra fees. (We’re confident you’ll love our service and never want to leave!)

Upgrades & Add-ons

Enhance your website anytime with upgrades or marketing campaigns like local SEO or Google Ads. Allow at least one month for setup before new campaigns launch or pages are added to your site.
